# 生成评分描述（scoringDescription）提示词模板

> 用途：把场景图 + 全量待用题目一次性喂给视觉大模型，产出**单段 150-220 字的评分用描述**，粘到 `demo-app/public/scenes/<场景id>/meta.json:scoringDescription`（v0.2.6 起支持多场景，每个场景各自一份）。
> 当 `public/config.json:demo.imageStrategy = "descriptionOnly"` 时，App 不再发图，只发这段描述 + 问题 + 学生答案给评分模型（`buildBatchEvaluationPrompt`），链路更快、Local 5min→~60s。
> 传图兜底档（`imageStrategy="image"`）不受影响，超时已放大到 `Gemini 120s / Local 360s + 自动压缩 1024/0.72`。

---

## 一、填好后直接发给视觉大模型（支持看图：Gemini / GPT-4V / Qwen-VL / 本地 Gemma-4-vision）

**发送内容 = 本文件“模板正文” + 附件 `Scene01_image.jfif`**

### 模板正文（复制整段，替换 `{{QUESTIONS_BLOCK}}`）

```
You are a precise image describer for Chinese junior students (13-15, average level in mainland China). You will help generate the SCORING description that a downstream strict teacher LLM will use INSTEAD OF the image.

（受众说明：首行按场景目标人群修改，如 10-12 岁小学生用 "Chinese primary school students (10-12, average level in mainland China)"；与 meta.json 的 `audience` 字段保持一致，评分 prompt 会按场景注入该受众。）

Image: attached scene image (outdoor street market).

Questions to be used in this scene (ALL levels that may be asked — fill from Demo/Scene01_questions.txt, currently 13 questions):

{{QUESTIONS_BLOCK}}

For example, format the block exactly as:
Level1:
Q1. Look at the picture. What fruits can you see? Name at least three.
Q2. Look at the boy named Zhang. He is holding a camera. What is he doing?
Q3. What is the weather like in the picture? What makes you say that?
Level2:
Q1. What are the students (Li Wei, Sarah, Chen, Zhang) doing now?
Q2. Look at the people. Are they happy? Is the market busy?
Q3. Look at the signs in the market. What other things can people buy here besides fruits?
Q4. Look at the dragon fruit, oranges, and bananas. Which one do you think is the most popular? Why?
Q5. There are many people in the market. Where do you think they are going?
Level3:
Q1. Imagine you are Li Wei. How would you recommend a local fruit (like dragon fruit or oranges) to Sarah? (You can start with: "You should try...")
Q2. If you are the store owner, how would you sell your fruits to the students?
Q3. What are the good things and bad things about street markets? What do you like and dislike?
Q4. Sarah and Chen are laughing. What do you think they are talking about?
Q5. You only have 20 yuan. What will you buy at this market? Why?

Task: Write ONE scoring-oriented description paragraph (150-220 English words, single paragraph, no bullets, no markdown headings) that covers ALL question-relevant evidence visible in the image. It is the ONLY visual source for the downstream scorer, so be exhaustive but concise.

Must cover if visible:
- Fruits & stalls: oranges, bananas, dragon fruits, watermelons, apples and their pile/position; stall signs
- People & actions: Li Wei (14), Sarah (14), Chen (15) at fruit stall — who holds what, who smiles/laughs; Zhang with camera — posture and intent
- Mood & scene: crowd, busy, happy, vendor/shopper activity
- Signs & other goods: Fresh Market, Bakery, Fish Market, Clothes, Handicrafts, Toys & Gifts
- Weather evidence: sun, blue sky, white clouds, shadows, flags, clothing
- Market pros/cons cues and shopping context (20 yuan scenario)

Rules:
- Be factual and grounded. Do not invent. If unclear, say "appears to be".
- Use junior-level English. No teaching, no scoring, no praise.
- Output ONLY JSON with a single key: {"scoringDescription": "your paragraph here"}
- The paragraph inside JSON must be 150-220 words, single paragraph, pasted as one string.

Now describe the image for these questions and output the JSON.
```

### {{QUESTIONS_BLOCK}} 占位说明
- 直接复制 `english-learning-app/Demo/Scene01_questions.txt` 全文，或按当前 `questions.json` 已选题目列表替换。
- 新增场景时，替换成新场景的题目即可复用本模板（v0.2.6 已用于 scene02「Yoyo's Birthday Party」，10-12 小学生受众）。

---

## 二、贴回 App

1. 视觉大模型返回示例：
```json
{"scoringDescription": "A vibrant outdoor street market on a sunny day. In the foreground ... colorful flags decorate the street and shoppers wear short sleeves, indicating warm sunny weather."}
```
2. 把 `scoringDescription` 的值原样粘到：
```
demo-app/public/scenes/scene01/meta.json
  → "scoringDescription": "（粘这里）"
```
3. 切换开关：
```json
// demo-app/public/config.json
"demo": { "imageStrategy": "descriptionOnly" } // 日常走描述，快速
"demo": { "imageStrategy": "image" }           // 兜底走图，保真（自动压缩 1024/0.72已内置）
```
4. 无需手动压图——`src/demo/usePresetSession.ts` 在 `image` 模式会自动 `canvas` 压缩。

---

## 三、校验

- 描述段长度 `150-220` 词，`JSON.parse` 能过，`meta.json` 校验通过。
- 切 `descriptionOnly` 后跑一次批量 3 题，看 `logs/model-log-*.txt` 为 `[local/batch]` 或 `[gemini/batch]` 且 `imageBase64=null`、`promptPreview` 含 `scoringDescription`，`elapsed` 应显著低于传图档（Local 60s 内、Gemini 15s 内）。
