Level1:
Q1. The picture has four panels. Name the four seasons written in the picture.

Q2. What is the girl doing in the spring panel? What is she holding?

Q3. What is the girl doing in the summer panel? What is she wearing on her head?

Q4. Read the weather words in each panel. Which season is "warm"? Which is "cold"?

Level2:

Q1. In the spring panel, the sun is smiling. In the summer panel, the sun is wearing sunglasses. Why are the two suns different?

Q2. Look at the autumn panel. What colour are the leaves on the ground? What is the girl putting into the basket?

Q3. In the winter panel, what is the girl wearing to keep warm? Name two things.

Q4. The summer panel has a thermometer. Is the temperature high or low? How do you know?

Q5. Match each activity to the season: fly a kite, go swimming, pick apples, make a snowman.

Level3:

Q1. Which of the four seasons do you like best? Why?

Q2. What do you usually do on a rainy day? Is it different from what you do on a sunny day?

Q3. Why do we wear different clothes in summer and winter? Give one example.

Q4. If you could add a fifth season, what would the weather be like? What would you do?

Level4:

Q1. Role-play: You are the girl in the summer panel. A friend from a cold country asks, "What is summer like in China?" Tell him or her in at least three sentences.

Q2. Role-play: You are a TV weather reporter. Tell the audience what the weather will be like tomorrow. Use "It will be..." and "You should wear..."

Q3. Role-play: The girl and her mum are talking about what to wear to school tomorrow. It is winter. Write the short conversation.
