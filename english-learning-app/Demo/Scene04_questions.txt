Level1:
Q1. Look at the town map. What places can you see? Name at least four.

Q2. Who are the people in the picture? What is the girl doing?

Q3. What is the girl wearing? What animal is beside her?

Q4. Read the notice on the map board. When is the library open?

Level2:

Q1. Look at the red route. Which place does it go through to get to the library?

Q2. Yuanyuan wants to go along Green Street to the library. How should she go? Use "Go straight" and "turn left/right" in your answer.

Q3. Which route to the library is shorter, the red one or the blue one? How do you know?

Q4. Today is Monday. What problem will Yuanyuan have when she gets to the library?

Q5. Now it is 5:30 in the afternoon. Can Yuanyuan borrow books? Why not?

Level3:

Q1. Which route do you like to walk to the library, the one through the park or the one along Green Street? Why?

Q2. Do you still need paper maps like this town map when you have a phone? Why?

Q3. How do you get to school every day? Talk about one street or one turn on your way.

Q4. Yuanyuan asks the police officer for help. Why is asking a police officer a good idea when you are lost in a city?

Level4:

Q1. Role-play: You are the police officer. Yuanyuan asks, "Excuse me, how can I get to the library?" Tell her the way in at least three sentences. Use "Go straight" and "turn left" or "turn right".

Q2. Role-play: You are Yuanyuan. Ask the police officer the way to the zoo. What do you say? (You can start with: "Excuse me...")

Q3. Role-play: A friend from another school visits your city. Tell him or her two interesting places in this town and how to get to one of them.
