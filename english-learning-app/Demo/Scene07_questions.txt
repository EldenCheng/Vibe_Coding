Level1:
Q1. Read the yellow price board. How much are apples? How much are bananas? How much is a watermelon?

Q2. Look at the girl's shopping list. What three fruits does she need to buy?

Q3. What is in the shopping cart? Name the fruits you can see.

Q4. How much money is the girl holding? Read the number on the banknote.

Level2:

Q1. The girl wants one kilo of apples and one kilo of bananas. How much do they cost together? (Use the price board.)

Q2. The girl has 20 yuan. Apples cost 5 and bananas cost 6. How much money will she have left after buying both?

Q3. Can the girl buy all three fruits on her list with 20 yuan? Why or why not?

Q4. What is the shopkeeper doing with the apple? What tool is he using?

Q5. Look at the girl's thought bubble. What is she thinking about? How is it different from what is in her cart?

Level3:

Q1. Do you like going shopping with your parents? What do you usually buy?

Q2. If you had 20 yuan at this supermarket, what would you buy? Explain your choice.

Q3. Why do people make a shopping list before they go to the supermarket?

Q4. The watermelon costs 12 yuan. Do you think that is cheap or expensive? Why?

Level4:

Q1. Role-play: You are the girl. Ask the shopkeeper: "How much are the apples?" Then ask, "Can I have two kilos, please?"

Q2. Role-play: You are the shopkeeper. The girl asks about the price of bananas. Answer her and ask, "How many kilos do you want?"

Q3. Role-play: You are the girl. You are talking to Mum on the phone. Tell her what you have bought and how much money is left.
