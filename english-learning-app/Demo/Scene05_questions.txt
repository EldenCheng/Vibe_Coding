Level1:
Q1. Look at the Chore Chart. What job is written next to "Mei"? What job is next to "Dad"?

Q2. What is Mei doing in the picture? What is Mum doing?

Q3. What is Grandpa doing? What is the cat doing?

Q4. Look at the floor near Mei. What two things can you see on the floor?

Level2:

Q1. The Chore Chart says "Mum - Clean." What is Mum actually cleaning in the picture?

Q2. Look at the icons on the Chore Chart. Which icon goes with "Dad - Cook"? Which goes with "Grandpa - Rest"?

Q3. Why is Grandpa not doing any housework today? What does the Chore Chart say about him?

Q4. Is the cat helping with the housework? Where is the cat?

Q5. Has Mei finished sweeping? How do you know?

Level3:

Q1. What housework do you do at home on the weekend? Name two things.

Q2. Do you think children should do housework? Why or why not?

Q3. In Mei's family, four people each have a job. Who do you think has the easiest job? Why?

Q4. If you could add one more job to this Chore Chart, what would it be? Who should do it?

Level4:

Q1. Role-play: You are Mum. Tell Mei what to do. Start with: "Mei, please..."

Q2. Role-play: Your friend calls and asks, "What is your family doing this weekend?" Tell him or her about the Chore Chart. Use "I can..." and "My dad can..."

Q3. Role-play: Mei says to Grandpa, "Grandpa, why are you resting?" Grandpa answers. Write the short conversation.
