Level1:
Q1. Look at the big poster on the wall. What is the title? What four health words can you see on it?

Q2. Look at the sick boy. What is on his head? What is he holding in his hand?

Q3. Look at the doctor. What is he wearing? What is he holding up?

Q4. Look at the shelf behind the doctor. What words can you see on the three medicine boxes?

Level2:

Q1. What's the matter with the boy? Name his health problem and give two pieces of evidence from the picture.

Q2. Read the green sign on the wall. What two pieces of advice can you see?

Q3. The boy has a bad cough. Which medicine box on the shelf should he take? How do you know?

Q4. Look at the four small pictures on the poster. Which picture shows FEVER? Which picture shows TOOTHACHE?

Q5. Do you think the boy should go to school tomorrow? Why or why not?

Level3:

Q1. When you have a cold, what do you usually do? Do you drink more water or take medicine?

Q2. The poster shows four health problems. Which one is the most common among your classmates? Why do you think so?

Q3. Many students do not like going to the doctor. Why do you think they feel that way? What can we do to help them?

Q4. The poster shows a boy holding his mouth for TOOTHACHE. Does the sick boy in the room have a toothache? How do you know?

Level4:

Q1. Imagine you are the doctor. The boy says "I don't feel well." Ask him two questions and give him two pieces of advice. (You can start with: "What's the matter?")

Q2. You are the sick boy. Tell the doctor how you feel and answer his questions.

Q3. Your friend has a toothache but he is afraid of the dentist. Talk to your friend and give him two reasons to see the dentist.
