Level1:
Q1. Look at the red banner at the top. What does it say?

Q2. What can you see hanging above the street? What color are they?

Q3. What are the performers in the center doing? Describe the dragon.

Q4. Look at the food stall. What food can you see? Name at least two kinds.

Level2:

Q1. Read the riddle board. The answer is in the picture. What is it? How do you know?

Q2. How much is a basket of dumplings? How much is one tanghulu? How much are they together?

Q3. How do you know it is a winter day? Give two pieces of evidence from the picture.

Q4. Look at the people behind the girl. Who is clapping? What are they watching?

Q5. Do you enjoy this kind of street fair? Why or why not?

Level3:

Q1. The girl in the red coat is eating a tanghulu with her eyes closed. How does she feel? What tells you that?

Q2. Which tradition in the picture do you like best? Why?

Q3. Some people say traditional festivals are becoming less popular among young people. Do you agree? Give two reasons.

Q4. What does your family usually do at Spring Festival? Is there anything in the picture that your family also does?

Level4:

Q1. Imagine you are the stall owner. A foreign visitor asks what tanghulu is. Sell it in three sentences. (You can start with: "Try this...")

Q2. Imagine you are a dragon dance performer. Your foreign teacher wants to try it. Tell him two things he should do. (You can start with: "Don't worry. First...")

Q3. Your class will run a stall at next year's fair. Describe your stall: what will you sell, and how will you make it special?
