Level1:
Q1. Read the big blue sign above the platform. Where is the train going? What time does it leave?

Q2. Look at the clock on the platform. What time is it now?

Q3. The suitcase is open. Name three things inside it.

Q4. What is the girl holding in her hands? Read the words on it.

Level2:

Q1. How much time do they have before the train leaves? How do you know?

Q2. Where is this family going on holiday - Beijing or Haikou? Give two pieces of evidence from the picture.

Q3. Look at the things in the suitcase: a swim ring, a camera, and a sun hat. Which item tells you they are going somewhere warm? Why?

Q4. Mum is holding a map. Why does she need a map on this trip?

Q5. The train has a smiling face. Is the train leaving right now? How do you know?

Level3:

Q1. Where did you go on your last holiday? How did you get there?

Q2. If you were going to Haikou, what three things would you put in your suitcase?

Q3. Why is it a good idea to arrive at the station before the train leaves?

Q4. Look at the suitcase. Do you think this family is going for a short trip or a long trip? Why?

Level4:

Q1. Role-play: You are the girl. You are very excited. Tell your class on the phone: "We are going to...!" Say at least three sentences.

Q2. Role-play: You are Mum. The train leaves in 30 minutes. Give two reminders to the family. Start with: "Hurry up! Don't forget..."

Q3. Role-play: After the holiday, your teacher asks, "Where did you go?" Answer in at least three sentences. Use "I went to..." and "I saw..."
