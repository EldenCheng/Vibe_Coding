Level1:
Q1. Look at the picture. What fruits can you see? Name at least three.

Q2. Look at the boy named Zhang. He is holding a camera. What is he doing?

Q3. What is the weather like in the picture? What makes you say that?

Level2:

Q1. What are the students (Li Wei, Sarah, Chen, Zhang) doing now?

Q2. Look at the people. Are they happy? Is the market busy?

Q3. Look at the signs in the market. What other things can people buy here besides fruits?

Q4. Look at the dragon fruit, oranges, and bananas. Which one do you think is the most popular? Why?

Q5. There are many people in the market. Where do you think they are going?

Level3:

Q1. Imagine you are Li Wei. How would you recommend a local fruit (like dragon fruit or oranges) to Sarah? (You can start with: "You should try...")

Q2. If you are the store owner, how would you sell your fruits to the students?

Q3. What are the good things and bad things about street markets? What do you like and dislike?

Q4. Sarah and Chen are laughing. What do you think they are talking about?

Q5. You only have 20 yuan. What will you buy at this market? Why?
