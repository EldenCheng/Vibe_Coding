Level1:
Q1. Look at the banner. What event is the school holding?

Q2. Look at the scoreboard. Which race does it show? What is the next event?

Q3. What are the three runners wearing? What numbers can you see?

Q4. Look at the left side. What is the girl in the navy tracksuit doing?

Level2:

Q1. Who won the 100m race? What was his time? Give two pieces of evidence.

Q2. How much faster was Li Hua than Zhang Peng?

Q3. Wang Fang finished third. Is her time bigger or smaller than Li Hua's? What does a bigger time mean in a race?

Q4. How does the runner with number 1 feel at the finish line? How do you know?

Q5. Does your school hold a sports meet? What is your favourite event there?

Level3:

Q1. Look at the high jump girl. Has she cleared the bar, or does she hit it? How do you know?

Q2. Why do schools hold sports meets? What can students learn from them? Give two ideas.

Q3. Some students think winning is the most important thing in sports. Do you agree? Why or why not?

Q4. Which do you enjoy more, individual sports like the 100m race or team sports like the relay? Why?

Level4:

Q1. Imagine you are Li Hua. A school reporter interviews you after the race. Tell him or her how the race went and how you felt.

Q2. Your friend fell during a race and wants to give up sports. Talk to him and give him two reasons to keep trying. (You can start with: "I know you are sad, but...")

Q3. Your class will design a poster for next year's sports meet. What three things will you put on it? Why?
